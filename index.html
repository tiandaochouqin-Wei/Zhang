Introduction

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Karolinska Institute, Stockholm, Sweden, working on medical image analysis, deep learning, computer vision, and multimodal learning. I received my PhD from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), China.

Over the years, my research has spanned several areas. My early work focused on environment perception for unmanned surface vehicles (USVs) through vision & LiDAR fusion, and object detection / tracking in maritime environments. I later collaborated on multimodal generation and vision transformers, with publications at ICCV, ECCV, IEEE TNNLS, and ACM Computing Surveys. My current research emphasis is on medical image computing — including low-dose CT denoising, PET/CT image fusion and quality assessment, brain-tumor segmentation and detection, federated learning for medical imaging, and capsule-endoscopy data compression.

I have published over 53 papers in international conferences and journals, including ICCV, ECCV, MICCAI, ICASSP, ICME, BIBM, PRCV, TrustCom, and journals such as IEEE TNNLS, ACM Computing Surveys, IEEE Access, Knowledge-Based Systems, Expert Systems with Applications, Scientific Data, ACM TOMM, and others.

I am committed to open science — releasing code, datasets, and benchmarks whenever possible. Researchers and clinicians interested in medical AI and translational biomedical engineering are warmly welcome to contact me.
